# Love Yourself Product Map

## Purpose of this document

This is the source of truth for what Love Yourself does, the value it provides,
and how it works.

Use only the relevant facts from this map. Explain them in the user's language
and at the level of detail needed for the specific question. Do not recite the
whole document or invent missing details. If the map and current context do not

---

## 1. What Love Yourself is

Love Yourself adds a short, concrete action to the workday at a time the user
chooses in advance.

The basic 7-day format has one moment per working day. The 14-day format adds a
second moment closer to the end of the working day.

Each action takes between 30 seconds and 3 minutes. It is not an arbitrary task
that happens to be short: every exercise uses a specific way to switch through
movement, breathing, attention, or a brief unload of thoughts.

The user chooses the time once, and the action is already prepared. They do not
have to decide again in the moment when to pause or what to do.

---

## 2. The value of an action

When attention becomes scattered or work reaches a dead end, a short action
moves focus from several competing inputs to one concrete step.

Unlike an open-ended feed, another browser tab, or aimless downtime, each action
has a clear beginning and end. Within 30 seconds or a few minutes, the user
completes one bounded moment of switching and returns to the day instead of
remaining inside an open-ended stream of distraction.

Over time, regular short pauses help people sustain their work rhythm for
longer, focus more deeply, and feel less depleted by the end of the day.

---

## 3. Exercise formats

The product uses two main ways of acting:

- briefly switch the body or attention through movement, breathing, cold water,
  or focus on a specific sensation;
- unload what has accumulated in the mind through a short written action closer
  to the end of the working day.

The user receives one concrete action, not a menu of alternatives.

---

## 4. The first 7 days

The first format always lasts 7 working days.

The user receives one short action on each working day at the chosen time.
Exercises are generated in advance according to defined selection rules, so the
user does not have to choose what to do each day.

---

## 5. What happens after 7 days

After completion, the user receives a short summary showing how many actions
were completed during the 7 working days.

Two options are then available:

- another 7 working days in the same format;
- 14 working days with two short actions: one during the day and another closer
  to the end of the working day.

---

## 6. Who can see what

The user can see their own activity and exercise completion pattern in the
weekly summary.

The company can see only anonymized, aggregated data about how the team uses
Love Yourself as a whole.

The company cannot see the user's conversation, the exercises they received,
whether they completed or skipped a specific exercise, or their individual
responses.

This is a core system principle, not an optional setting.

---

## 7. What the Coach takes into account

The Coach receives the current conversation and the available system context
about the current 7 or 14 days so it can respond consistently without starting
every conversation from zero.

The conversation, the user's mood, and completion of a previous exercise do not
change an exercise sequence that has already been generated.

---

## 8. How exercises are selected

The exercise sequence is prepared in advance according to defined product
rules. The user does not need to select exercises, manage variety, or decide
what is more suitable for a daytime or evening moment; the system has already
taken care of that.

The rules preserve the logic of the sequence, separate daytime and evening
purposes, and prevent the same exercise from appearing too often.

Once the current 7 or 14 days have been created, the sequence is not rebuilt
after each message, missed exercise, or completed exercise.

---

## 9. Why this action is shown now

It is the next exercise in the sequence already generated for the current 7 or
14 days. It appears now because the time selected by the user during setup has
arrived.

The user controls delivery time. If the current time no longer works, it can be
changed and future exercises will arrive at the new time.

The specific exercise is not selected as an assessment of the user's mood or
psychological state.

---

## 10. Why these exercises

Exercises are intentionally short and simple so they can realistically be done
in the middle of a working day without taking the user away from it for long.

Even a short action moves attention away from the work stream and onto one
concrete step, creating one bounded switching point within the working day.

---

## 11. Available actions: pause, cancel, and change time

**Pause** temporarily stops exercise delivery. The generated sequence is
preserved. After resuming, it continues with the next day.

**Cancel** permanently ends the current 7 or 14 days. That same sequence cannot
be resumed, but the user can start a new one later. The selected time and other
settings remain, so onboarding does not need to be repeated. The user does not
receive a progress summary for the canceled 7 or 14 days.

The format cannot be changed from 7 to 14 days or from 14 to 7 days while the
current 7 or 14 days are still running. To switch formats before completion,
the user must cancel the current one and start a new one in the desired format.

**Change time** moves future exercises to a new time selected by the user.

Pause is reversible. Cancellation is final for the current 7 or 14 days.

---

## 12. If a day is missed

Missing a day does not shift or rebuild the sequence.

If the current 7 or 14 days remain active, the next exercise arrives at the
chosen time on the next working day regardless of how many earlier exercises
were missed.

If exercises temporarily do not fit the user's working day, the user can change
the time or pause delivery.

---

## 13. If an exercise does not fit the current moment

An exercise is optional. The user can skip it when they do not have the time,
ability, or desire to do it, or when their surroundings make it impractical.

The next exercise will arrive according to schedule. If the problem repeatedly
comes from inconvenient timing, the time can be changed. If the user needs a
break from all exercises, they can pause delivery.

---

## 14. If an exercise did not have a noticeable effect

Not every short action feels equally noticeable.

Its value is not limited to an immediate feeling: the action has clear
boundaries, moves attention to one concrete step, and ends within 30 seconds or
a few minutes.

One day does not build a lasting habit, just as one workout does not change
physical fitness. Regularity over a period of weeks helps people manage
attention and energy more effectively, sustain their work rhythm for longer,
and feel less depleted by the end of the day.

---

## 15. Conversation outside the schedule

The user can write to the Coach at any time.

The bot initiates only one moment per day in the 7-day format or two moments per
day in the 14-day format. Every bot-initiated message interrupts the user's
current activity, so these moments are intentionally limited.

When the user writes first, no additional interruption is created. The Coach
can answer questions about Love Yourself, exercises, and available actions or
support the user in the context of their working day.
