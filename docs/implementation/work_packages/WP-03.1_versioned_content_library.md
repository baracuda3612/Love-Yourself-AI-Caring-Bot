## Release eligibility and text fallback

Define eligibility in the single content-read boundary and apply it to every new scheduled or on-demand selection and delivery entrance:

```text
eligible = is_active
           AND (NOT review_required OR review_status == approved)
           AND (NOT instructional_gif_required OR valid_approved_own_gif)
           AND (NOT media_attached OR valid_approved_own_gif)

valid_approved_own_gif = own_gif.exists
                         AND own_gif.approved_for(exercise_id, content_version)
                         AND own_gif.version_and_integrity_valid
                         AND descriptive_alt_text.exists

instructional_gif_required = exercise_id IN
    {breathing_sigh, pmr_fist, cold_water_face}
```

An asset approval is separate from clinical review. `review_status=unreviewed` does **not** exclude the eight records whose `review_required=false` for a limited beta once any required or attached media passes approval and integrity validation; the other six need no GIF. The cool-water record and GIF are prepared in WP-03.1, but the exercise cannot be selected or delivered until a later qualified medical review approves the exact protocol; its GIF approval alone does not pass `DG-02`. If medical review changes steps or safety copy, publish the corrected content version and approve the matching instructional GIF before eligibility. No fallback to a legacy parent, another exercise's media, or a shared generic loop is allowed. Text-only release is the intended contract for the six exercises without required media, not an exception for the three instructional records. The six-`switch` source pool should yield five eligible on-demand records without cool water once required media passes; WP-06.1 owns actual on-demand selection and occurrence behavior.

The exercise text remains authoritative and fully usable when Telegram cannot send, load, or play an approved GIF. Media delivery failure for an already selected eligible exercise must fall back to that same exercise's complete text and available action; it must not reselect, mark the content ineligible retroactively, or lose the action. WP-03.1 supplies and validates this payload and a narrow read contract. WP-03.3 implements the canonical `ExercisePresentation`, channel rendering, Telegram GIF/text fallback, and actual delivery-variant snapshot. An instructional GIF may show the protocol's real tempo, including `pmr_fist`'s 5-second hold and 5-second release/pause. It is not an interactive or authoritative timer and cannot replace complete text instructions.
